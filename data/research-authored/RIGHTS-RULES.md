# Rights determination rules — San Gorgonio Pass photo catalog

Every record gets `rights_status` ∈ {Copyrighted, Permission required, Public domain, Unclear}
plus a `rights_note` naming the holder / institution / rationale / concrete next action.
Applied campaign-wide 2026-07-21. US copyright law as of 2026 (95-year publication rule:
works published 1930 or earlier are public domain as of Jan 1, 2026).

Order of application (first rule that fits):

1. **Calisphere-matched records** — capture the item's Rights Information statement verbatim
   in evidence. "Copyright status unknown" boilerplate → `Unclear`, note: "Calisphere/BLD
   rights statement: copyright status unknown. Next action: request reproduction permission
   from the contributing institution (Beaumont Library District / Banning Library District),
   which is standard practice for these collections." A named rights holder in the statement
   (e.g. Laura May Stewart Trust) → `Copyrighted` with that holder named.
2. **Published pre-1931 material** (commercial postcards, newspaper pages, promotional
   brochures demonstrably published before 1931) → `Public domain`: "Published before 1931;
   U.S. copyright expired (95-year term)." Name the collection the scan came from as courtesy
   credit, not as rights holder.
3. **U.S. federal government works** (NASA, U.S. Navy photographs) → `Public domain`
   (17 U.S.C. § 105), credit the agency/photographer.
4. **Google Street View / Google Maps captures** → `Copyrighted` — Google LLC. Not usable in
   a printed mural without Google's permission; recommend replacing with an original photo.
5. **Named newspaper clippings 1931+** (Record-Gazette, etc.) → `Copyrighted` — the
   publisher; contact the paper (Record-Gazette, Banning, CA) for reproduction rights.
6. **Named individual photographers / private collections still in copyright**
   (Pat Murkland, Frasher Foto (Pomona Public Library holds the collection), Karen Sumner
   Noordman, Steve Lech, Scott Deforge, family snapshots 1931+) → `Copyrighted` (photographer
   known) or `Permission required` (collection owner known, photographer not) — name the
   person/institution as contact.
7. **Institutional physical collections without a copyright claim** (Beaumont Library
   District, Banning Library District, SGPHS, Huntington Library, Malki Museum) →
   `Permission required` — the institution controls reproduction of its physical holdings
   even where underlying copyright has expired; name the institution.
8. **Family collections, unpublished, photographer unknown** (Estrada, Stewart) —
   created before ~1906 → `Public domain` (unpublished, anonymous: 120 years from creation);
   1906+ → `Permission required` — the family/donor via BLD.
9. **Modern unattributed photos from client folders** → `Unclear`: "Photographer
   unidentified. Next action: BLD to confirm whether staff/volunteer work (then BLD-owned)
   or obtain photographer's permission."
10. **Anything else** → `Unclear` with a concrete, named next action.

Note on tier 2 vs 7: a pre-1931 *published* postcard is PD even if an institution holds the
scanned copy — institutions cannot claim new copyright in faithful reproductions of PD 2-D
works (Bridgeman v. Corel). Courtesy credit is still given. Institution permission is only a
*practical* requirement when we must re-source a better scan from them.
